# Customer Communication Guidelines for Disputes

> Fictional internal guidance written for the Sentinel training project.

## Tone

- Be clear, calm and specific. Avoid jargon such as "chargeback reason code" or "MCC".
- Never blame the customer. Do not suggest the customer was careless.
- Use the customer's preferred channel recorded on file.

## Required content in outcome letters

1. The transaction(s) under review: date, merchant and amount.
2. The decision and what happens next (credit, card replacement, or no change).
3. The reason for the decision in one or two plain sentences.
4. How to ask for the documents the bank relied on, and how to appeal.

## Sensitive information

- Never include the full card number in any message. Use the last four digits only.
- Do not mention internal model scores, risk tiers or the existence of fraud models to customers.
- Do not mention AML referrals (see AML Escalation Guidelines).

## Response times

- Respond to secure messages within 1 business day.
- Call back customers whose card was blocked by the fraud system within 2 hours during business hours.
