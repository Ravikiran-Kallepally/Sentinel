# Regulation Z - Credit Card Billing Error Resolution (Educational Summary)

> Educational summary for the Sentinel training project. Not legal text and not legal advice. Refer to
> 12 CFR Part 1026 (Truth in Lending) and the compliance team.

## Scope

Regulation Z implements the Truth in Lending Act, including the Fair Credit Billing Act provisions that
govern credit card billing errors and unauthorized use. Sentinel Bank's card products are credit cards, so
most card disputes handled by investigators fall under Regulation Z rather than Regulation E.

## Liability for unauthorized use

A cardholder's liability for unauthorized use of a credit card is capped at $50. Sentinel Bank applies a
zero-liability policy for confirmed unauthorized use, so customers are not charged that $50.

## What counts as a billing error

Billing errors include charges the cardholder did not authorize, charges for goods or services not accepted
or not delivered as agreed, incorrect amounts, computational errors, failure to credit a payment or a
refund, and charges for which the cardholder requests documentation.

## Timelines

- The cardholder must send a billing error notice so that the creditor receives it within 60 days after the
  creditor transmitted the first periodic statement that reflected the error.
- The creditor must send a written acknowledgment within 30 days of receiving the notice, unless it
  resolves the dispute within that period.
- The creditor must resolve the dispute within two complete billing cycles, and in no case later than
  90 days after receiving the notice.

## During an investigation

- The creditor may not try to collect the disputed amount, including related finance charges, while the
  dispute is pending.
- The creditor may not report the disputed amount as delinquent to credit bureaus while it is pending.
- The disputed amount can remain on the statement, but the cardholder does not have to pay it until the
  dispute is resolved.

## Outcomes

If the creditor finds an error, it must correct the account and credit any related finance charges or fees.
If it finds no error, it must explain why in writing and, on request, provide documentary evidence.
